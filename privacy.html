2. What we collect, and why

Anonymous play analytics (on by default)

To understand how many people play and how the Game is doing, we collect a small amount of pseudonymous data tied to a random id stored in your browser — not to your real identity. This includes: a random device id, timestamps of play, the Game version, your browser’s timezone and language (used as a rough region indicator), device type (mobile/desktop), screen size, the page you arrived from, and basic gameplay totals (distance walked / driven / flown, badges earned). We do not collect your name, precise location, or any contact details through analytics.

You can turn this off at any time: open the Game, tap the 👤 Account button, and use “Opt out of analytics”. Opting out stops all analytics collection on your device.

Optional account (only if you choose to sign in)

Signing in is entirely optional and only used to save your progress across devices. If you sign in, we process your email address (and a display name if you set one) via our authentication provider, Supabase. We use your email solely to sign you in and secure your account; we do not send marketing email unless you explicitly opt in. You can sign out anytime and request deletion of your account and associated data (see Section 6).

Multiplayer (only while you play co-op)

When you start or join a co-op room, your chosen display name and your in-game position are shared in real time with the other players in that room so you can see each other move. This data is transient and is not stored after the session.

Data is stored using Supabase (PostgreSQL hosting and authentication), which acts as our data processor. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. We retain anonymous analytics for up to [e.g. 12 months] and account data until you delete your account.
